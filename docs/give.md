# Give

![A woven offering basket beside a terraced hillside](assets/img/give-light.webp)

LaoChristian.org is a ministry of the Lao Holland Seventh-day Adventist
Church, supported entirely by the generosity of people who believe in making
Scripture and worship freely available in Lao.

## Online giving

Seventh-day Adventist churches accept tithe and offerings online through
[AdventistGiving](https://adventistgiving.org/), the denomination's official
giving platform. Search for **Lao Holland** to give directly to our
congregation.

Use a limited palette, now in a deep-night register: deep night blue #102B34 and blue charcoal #172F35 as the dominant dark field, with deep Mekong green #173B38, forest teal #2E675E, muted sage #789B83, and champa gold #E5B957 as warm lamp-light accents.

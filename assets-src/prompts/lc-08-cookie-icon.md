# LC-08 — Cookie consent icon

Not from the original brief — a small icon graphic for the cookie-consent
banner, replacing the old site's generic clipart cookie mascot.

Used for: cookie consent banner (site-wide popup).

**Single image (works on either color scheme — see notes):** A single round
Lao/Western-style shortbread cookie with a bite taken out, rendered as a
gentle paper-cut/gouache illustration, sitting on plain rice-paper. A tiny
dok champa flower petal or two beside it as a quiet accent. Square
composition, the cookie centered with generous surrounding negative space so
it crops cleanly to a small circular or rounded-square icon.

**Meaning:** Warm, approachable, not corporate or legalistic — the small
compliance moment should still feel like part of the same handcrafted world
as the rest of the site.

**Avoid:** Cookie-cutter (pun intended) stock-clipart look, browser-cookie
puns rendered literally (no computer chips, no browser UI), text of any
kind, and anything overly cute/cartoonish that clashes with the site's
restrained tone.

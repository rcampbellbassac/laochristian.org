Source: same ChatGPT design-brief conversation as LC-01/LC-02. Generated images
used on the homepage's Sieng Khaen Lao section: `worship-light.webp` /
`worship-dark.webp` (originals archived at
`assets-src/raw/lc-03-worship-khaen-light.png` / `-dark.png`).

Used for: Website Worship/Music section, Sieng Khaen Lao page, future app Lao
Songs collection art, worship navigation card, audio-player background.

**Light:** Vertical khaen reeds rise like a restrained architectural rhythm
beside a river. Subtle musical curves move through terraced fields. A small
gathering area or open pavilion may appear in the distance without becoming the
focal point.

**Dark:** An evening riverside village during quiet worship. Several warm lights
form a gentle musical rhythm along the water. Khaen reeds and one musical note
appear as subtle paper-cut foreground elements beneath a starry sky.

**Meaning:** Worship, Lao musical heritage, congregation, and shared faith.

**Avoid:** Same restrictions as LC-02 — no temples/monks/Buddhist imagery, no
national flags, no photorealistic stock look.

# LC-05 — Community & Events

Not from the original brief — drafted following the same format, for the
Events page hero and event-listing cards.

Used for: Events page hero background, event card art.

**Light:** A simple open-air wooden pavilion (sala) at the edge of a
terraced hillside, empty but clearly prepared for a gathering — a few low
cushions or floor mats arranged, a strand of small paper lanterns strung
along the eaves. Late-afternoon light. River and mountains calm in the
distance.

**Dark:** The same pavilion at early evening. The strand of lanterns is now
lit with warm light, gently illuminating the empty seating. Terraces fade to
shadow; the river catches the last blue light.

**Meaning:** Gathering, fellowship, and anticipation — a place prepared for
the community to come together — deliberately left empty of people so it
doesn't read as one specific past event.

**Avoid:** Specific identifiable people or crowds, literal church
architecture, balloons/confetti/party imagery, Western event-decor clichés.
Distinct from LC-03 (which shows a populated night worship gathering) by
staying unpopulated.

## Generation log

First pass (gpt-image-2, low quality) came back with a pavilion roof with
upturned/curved finials -- reads as Buddhist temple ("wat") architecture
even without any explicit temple content, which the brief explicitly wants
avoided. Caught in review, revised the prompt to explicitly require a plain
straight-gabled roof and call out "no chofah bird-tail ornaments," and
regenerated. Final images: docs/assets/img/events-light.webp / -dark.webp.

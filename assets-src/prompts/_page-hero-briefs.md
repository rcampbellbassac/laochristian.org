# Remaining page-hero briefs (LC-09 through LC-18)

Drafted following the same LC-XX format as LC-04-07, each distinct from
existing motifs (open Bible = hero/Bible, khaen reeds = worship, book stack
= study, pavilion+lanterns = events, rice basket = give, empty path = about).

- **LC-09 Resources hub** — a simple wooden trellis/gate archway at the edge
  of a terrace, champa vine growing over it, opening onto the river/mountain
  view beyond. Meaning: a gateway into everything the site offers.
- **LC-10 Audio** — reeds and grass bending in a breeze beside the river,
  gentle ripples on the water surface suggesting sound, one bird in flight
  at a distance. No equipment/technology of any kind.
- **LC-11 Sermons** — a simple rustic wooden stand/lectern (not a pulpit or
  church furniture) with one open book on it, alone on the terrace, catching
  light. Suggests proclamation without literal church architecture.
- **LC-12 Studies** — an open notebook and a simple pen beside a small cup,
  on the stone ledge, soft light. Personal reflection/note-taking, distinct
  from the general book-stack motif already used for "study resources."
- **LC-13 Books** — a small handful of books tied with plain twine, as if
  bundled to share, resting on the ledge. Distinct from the larger stack
  already used on the Written-resources hub.
- **LC-14 Contact** — a lit lantern beside a simple wooden gate/doorway at
  dusk, a path leading up to it. Welcoming, "come in."
- **LC-15 Apps** — a simple woven satchel/bag resting on the ledge, slightly
  open, a book peeking out. "Faith to carry with you" without depicting a
  phone or screen.
- **LC-16 Live** — a single tall lantern/beacon, brightly lit, on the
  hilltop at dusk. Signals "happening now" without cameras or screens.
- **LC-17 Privacy** — a closed book resting safely half-tucked under a leaf
  or beside a woven basket lid. Quiet, protective, restrained.
- **LC-18 Cookies** — a wider landscape composition of the same cookie
  scene as the consent-banner icon (lc-08), for visual continuity between
  the small popup and the page's own hero.

All must-avoid list carries over from `_style-preamble.md`, plus: no temple-
style upturned roof finials on any structure (gate, lectern, pavilion),
same guardrail added after the Events regeneration caught it.

The visual direction is "Quiet Lao Modernism": contemporary editorial minimalism, disciplined grid structure, Japanese restraint and negative space, mid-century shape economy, and refined layered-paper illustration in a soft gouache/watercolor style, without imitating any specific designer. Peaceful, culturally Lao, Scripture-centered, handcrafted but not rustic, modern but not technological, distinctly Lao without resembling tourism advertising.

Use a limited palette, now in a deep-night register: deep night blue #102B34 and blue charcoal #172F35 as the dominant dark field, with deep Mekong green #173B38, forest teal #2E675E, muted sage #789B83, and champa gold #E5B957 as warm ambient-light accents. Add only faint paper grain and delicate layer shadows -- no glossy 3D rendering, glassmorphism, neon colors, or excessive gradients.

Scene: the same simple woven bamboo offering basket on a low stone ledge beside a terraced Lao hillside, now at dusk, filled with rice grains and a few scattered dok champa petals. Soft warm ambient light falls gently across the basket -- not from an ornate lamp, just enough light to see the rice and petals. Terraces fade into soft blue shadow. Limestone mountains are a dark silhouette in the distance, the river catching the last light of dusk.

Composition: wide landscape orientation, generous negative space in the upper sky area for later text overlay, the basket positioned in the lower-left third, the dark mountains and river filling the right two-thirds. No text, no logos, no UI elements of any kind rendered in the image.

Avoid entirely: temples, stupas, monks, Buddha imagery, elephants or tourism imagery, the Lao national flag, photorealistic stock-photo look, gold ornamental borders, currency, coins, cash, wallets, collection plates, offering boxes, any transactional or commercial imagery, oversized crosses, glowing supernatural effects, or any Adventist/denominational logo.

# Shared style preamble

Every generation prompt for laochristian.org art should open with this block
(or a close paraphrase), so images stay consistent even when generated via
API calls that don't share ChatGPT conversation context. Scene-specific
prompts (LC-04, LC-05, ...) add their own "Used for / Light / Dark / Meaning"
on top of this.

## Preamble text

The visual direction is "Quiet Lao Modernism": contemporary editorial
minimalism, disciplined grid structure, Japanese restraint and negative
space, mid-century shape economy, and refined layered-paper illustration
in a soft gouache/watercolor style, without imitating any specific designer.
Peaceful, culturally Lao, Scripture-centered, handcrafted but not rustic,
modern but not technological, distinctly Lao without resembling tourism
advertising.

Use a limited palette: rice-paper background #F6F1E7, deep Mekong green
#173B38, forest teal #2E675E, muted sage #789B83, dusty river blue #88A7B0,
champa gold #E5B957, and soft clay #B86F50. Warm ivory should be the
dominant field. Add only faint paper grain and delicate layer shadows — no
glossy 3D rendering, glassmorphism, neon colors, or excessive gradients.

Strongest motifs (use only two or three per image): limestone karst
mountains, the Mekong or a quiet river ribbon, rice-terrace contours, dok
champa flowers, bamboo or khaen-reed vertical rhythms, subtle Lao
woven-textile geometry, mist/dawn/twilight light.

Avoid entirely: temples, stupas, monks, Buddha imagery or Buddhist ornament,
elephants or generic tourism imagery, the Lao national flag, photorealistic
stock-photo look, gold ornamental borders, literal church-building
illustrations, oversized crosses, glowing "supernatural" effects, or any
Adventist/denominational logo (never draw or reinterpret the official
Adventist symbol — that space stays reserved and empty).

## Original brief prompt (verbatim)

> Create a premium desktop homepage concept for LaoChristian.org, presented as a
> polished 16:9 website design at 2560 × 1440. The visual direction is "Quiet Lao
> Modernism": contemporary editorial minimalism, disciplined Swiss-style grid
> structure, Japanese restraint and negative space, mid-century shape economy, and
> refined layered paper illustration, without imitating any specific designer.
>
> Use a warm, softly textured rice-paper background in #F6F1E7. Build the layout on
> seven vertical columns, with the first six used for content and a narrow
> contrasting seventh "Sabbath column" on the far right. Leave the Sabbath column
> mostly visual, using deep Mekong green and a very subtle paper texture. Reserve a
> small clean area for an official Adventist symbol to be added later; do not draw,
> reinterpret, or generate the Adventist logo.
>
> Create a minimal header with a clean LaoChristian.org wordmark area on the left,
> restrained navigation in the center, and one simple rounded rectangular action
> button on the right. Avoid floating pill navigation and excessive shadows.
>
> The hero section should contain a spacious text area on the left and an elegant
> layered-paper landscape on the right. Render only the exact main title
> "ລາວຄຣິສຕຽນ" and the English name "LaoChristian.org". Leave remaining text areas
> clean and editable; do not generate paragraphs of fake text.
>
> The landscape should feature simplified Lao limestone mountains, gentle mist,
> curving rice terraces, a winding Mekong-like river, a quiet dawn sun, and one
> restrained white-and-gold dok champa branch. Integrate a subtle path toward the
> horizon and a very understated open-Bible shape within the lower paper layers.
> The religious symbolism should feel peaceful and natural rather than literal or
> promotional.
>
> Below the hero, show three refined resource cards representing the Lao Bible, Lao
> worship songs, and Christian study resources. Use custom paper-cut icons: an open
> Bible, a musical symbol subtly combined with khaen-reed geometry, and a small
> collection of books. Keep the cards spacious, lightly bordered, and nearly flat,
> with restrained shadows and strong alignment.
>
> Use a limited palette: deep Mekong green #173B38, forest teal #2E675E, muted sage
> #789B83, dusty river blue #88A7B0, champa gold #E5B957, and soft clay #B86F50. Use
> warm ivory as the dominant field. Add only faint paper grain and delicate layer
> shadows.
>
> Do not include temples, stupas, monks, Buddha imagery, elephants, national flags,
> tourist-poster styling, photographic stock imagery, glossy three-dimensional
> rendering, glassmorphism, neon colors, excessive gradients, ornamental gold
> frames, clutter, crowded text, or malformed religious logos.

## What we actually used

The chat's full mockup renders (`lc-01-homepage-mockup-light/dark.png`) bake the
UI chrome directly into the image, so they're reference only — we built the real
header/cards/nav in HTML+CSS instead. The clean landscape-only renders
(LC-02, below) are what ship as the actual hero background, since they isolate
just the illustration with no fake UI to fight with real markup.

# LC-02 — Lao Bible (river bend, open Bible)

Used for: homepage hero background, will also anchor the future Bible resources
page.

**Light:** An open Bible resting naturally beside a quiet river bend. Morning
light crosses the pages. Faint terrace contours flow outward from the book like
lines of Scripture. Mountains remain restrained in the distance.

**Dark:** The same riverbank under soft moonlight. The Bible is illuminated by a
small, warm, non-ornamental reading lamp. Moonlight reflects on the river;
distant homes remain understated.

**Meaning:** Scripture as living water, guidance, stability, and light.

**Avoid:** Oversized crosses, glowing supernatural effects, floating pages, or
Western church architecture.

# LC-04 — Written & Study Resources

Not from the original ChatGPT brief (which only fully specified LC-02 and
LC-03) — drafted here following the same format so it stays consistent, for
use on the "Explore Christian Resources" card and the future written
resources hub/Studies/Sermons/Books pages.

Used for: Written-resources hub background, Explore Christian Resources
card, future Studies/Sermons/Books subpages.

**Light:** A small stack of well-worn books resting on a low stone ledge
beside a terraced hillside, one book left open with a simple ribbon marker.
Warm midday light. Modest ink-and-paper texture on the book spines in deep
green, clay, and sage tones echoing the palette. Mountains and river remain
distant and calm.

**Dark:** The same ledge at dusk. A single small oil lamp gives warm light
over the open book. Terrace contours fade into blue shadow; a few distant
window-lights suggest a quiet village.

**Meaning:** Study, reflection, and the accumulated written teaching of the
church — sermons, Bible studies, and books — as a steady, unhurried
practice, distinct from LC-02's single open Scripture.

**Avoid:** Cluttered piles, generic "library" imagery, Western academic
robes/mortarboards, or any literal classroom setting.
